## Decisions Made

- Migration renamed from `0005_slimy_goliath` to `0005_decision_traces_unique_decision_id` following the pattern established in Phase 08-02 — drizzle-kit tracks by journal tag, rename is safe, snapshot is numerically indexed and unaffected
- `MAX_INVOCATIONS_PER_BOT = 50` caps LLM cost per bot execution; rejected tool calls are deliberately included because counterfactual analysis (CNCL-04) needs the full call history including rejected attempts
- `reasoning_branch` writes a row even on LLM failure — a low-confidence fallback row (confidence=0.000, outcome=partial) ensures the Council always has a reasoning_branch record to evaluate
- `output_step` rows are written without an LLM call — task delivery is structural, not directive-driven; `directiveReferenced` is null
- Verbatim directive validation degrades confidence to max 0.3 and sets `validationWarning: 'directive_not_verbatim_in_soul'` in metadata when the LLM cites a directive that does not appear verbatim in soulContent, addressing the hallucination risk documented in research
