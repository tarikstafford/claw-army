# PRD: Claw Army Landing Page — UI-Marketer

**Date:** February 19, 2026  
**Product:** Claw Army (working title)  
**Persona:** Growth-Hungry E-commerce Team

## 1. Objective

Ship a standalone landing page tailored to e-commerce growth teams seeking fast, controlled experimentation and lead generation. The page should drive strategy calls and campaign-idea lead capture.

## 2. Persona

### “Growth-Hungry Ecom Team”

- Founder, growth lead, or marketer at DTC/e-commerce brands.
- Pain points:
  - High CAC and stale campaign performance
  - Creative testing bottlenecks
  - Limited team bandwidth
- Motivations:
  - Launch many growth experiments quickly
  - Keep spend controlled and measurable

## 3. Positioning

**Claw Army = Parallel AI workforce with guardrails.**

Differentiators to communicate:
- Parallel task execution
- Budget and safety guardrails
- Tool Gateway + isolation security model
- Performance scoring / leaderboards
- DNA capture flywheel (system improves over time)

## 4. Messaging Strategy

- Tone: sharp, outcome-driven, credible, less playful than agent page.
- Hook options:
  - “Launch 50 growth experiments before your competitors launch 5.”
  - “Your AI guerrilla team for lead capture, creative testing, and outreach.”
  - “Scale growth ideas without scaling headcount.”

## 5. Look & Feel

- Visual direction: modern editorial + tactical growth dashboard
- Palette: warm neutrals with aggressive orange/lime accents
- Typography: premium serif headline + pragmatic sans body
- Motion: campaign cards sliding into pipeline, cost meter activity, lead ticker
- Desired emotional response: speed, ROI confidence, trust

## 6. Information Architecture

1. Hero (outcome statement + CTA)
2. Use-case grid (lead gen, UGC ideas, outreach angles, offer testing)
3. “How it works” campaign lifecycle
4. ROI/budget guardrail proof
5. Case-style scenario blocks
6. Security/trust section
7. Pricing/engagement model
8. FAQ + conversion CTA

## 7. CTA Strategy

- Primary CTA: **Book a Growth Strategy Call**
- Secondary CTA: **Get 10 Guerrilla Campaign Ideas**

## 8. Conversion + Analytics Requirements

Track:
- Hero CTA CTR
- Scroll depth
- Strategy call conversion rate
- Lead magnet conversion rate
- Time on page
- Section engagement (use-case cards, pricing, FAQ)
- Persona self-selection click rate (if cross-linking pages)

## 9. MVP Scope

- Standalone page for e-commerce persona
- Distinct copy, design system, and CTA flow
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Social proof placeholders
- Analytics events wired for funnel tracking

## 10. Non-Goals (v1)

- Full pricing calculator
- Complex product simulator
- Localization
- Deep case study library

## 11. Content Guardrails

- Avoid spam/black-hat language.
- Frame “guerrilla” as creative, rapid experimentation.
- Ensure any bold claim maps to real platform architecture/capabilities.

## 12. Launch Plan

1. Finalize e-commerce copy and voice.
2. Wireframe and prototype.
3. Build and ship v1.
4. A/B test hero + CTA variants.
5. Iterate using first two weeks of conversion data.
